- Retain required per-request price caps and cost estimates as scientific execution metadata. These are distinct from account balance records.
- Document historical code path adaptations and the new independent common-100/JSON-fence verification program. Historical scripts and results are distinguished from fresh checks.

Source-member action counts include duplicate records across overlapping original packages. They are not a count of unique questions or unique final files. The authoritative final inventory is `file_manifest.json`.
